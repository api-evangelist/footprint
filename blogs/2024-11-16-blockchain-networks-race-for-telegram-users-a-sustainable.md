Author: Stella L ( stella@footprint.network ) The blockchain gaming landscape in 2024 has been dramatically reshaped by an unexpected catalyst: Telegram. As blockchain networks scramble to capture users from this messaging platform’s massive user base, a critical question emerges: Is this strategy delivering sustainable growth, or merely creating temporary surges in vanity metrics? The Evolution of the Telegram Gaming Phenomenon The Telegram gaming revolution began with Notcoin’s simple tap-to-earn mechanic, which quickly proved that the messaging platform could be a powerful gateway to Web3.
