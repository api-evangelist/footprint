Growthly is a viral marketing tool that helps projects build sustainable growth through their existing user base. Author: Stella L ( stella@footprint.network ) Many Web3 projects consider Growthly to be just another quest platform. This misunderstanding stems from the prevalence of quest-based growth tools in the Web3 space.
